The card reads `--card`, `--card-foreground`, `--muted`, `--muted-foreground`, `--border` and `--background`, so it follows your theme in light and dark mode. Panels sit on `bg-muted/50`; the only colour comes from the `accent` gradient, which defaults to a soft mix of `--primary`.

Market mode converts with `pay.price / receive.price`. Limit mode replaces that rate with the editable target price, which starts at the market rate and can be reset with the "Market" chip. Output is `amountIn × rate × (1 − feeBps / 10_000)`, and minimum received applies the selected slippage tolerance on top.

Tokens with a `balance` get Half and Max chips and block the action with "Insufficient {symbol} balance" when the amount exceeds it. Logos are plain `<img>` elements, so any host works without `images.remotePatterns`; a missing or broken logo falls back to the symbol's first letter.

The receive value, rate and action status sit behind polite live regions. The tab row, picker, settings popover and details toggle expose their state through ARIA roles, the picker takes focus and traps Tab while open, Escape closes it and returns focus to the token button, and the amount fields are labelled decimal inputs. Toasts are not used.

The whole panel is wrapped in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`. With reduced motion enabled, the sliding pills, panel reordering, picker slide, limit row and details height changes all switch instantly, and the swap spinner stops.
