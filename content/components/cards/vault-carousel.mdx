The `VaultFeature` shape: `name` (card title), `tagline` (one-line summary under the title), `category` (used for the filter chips), `illustration` (one of the built-in `VaultArtKind` values), and the optional `chain` (chain badge), `since` (shown as "Since <year>" in the open panel), `metric` (`{ value, label }`), and `description` (detail panel body).

Built-in illustration kinds and their accent art: `shield-lock` (shield), `fingerprint` (phone), `face-id` (ID card), `multisig` (multisig), `vault` (safe), `key` (padlock), `radar` (radar), `robot` (robot). Each kind maps to a distinct isometric illustration.

Theming uses the `dark` class: the surface, ink, muted text and hairlines switch between light cream and dark slate palettes automatically.

Accessibility: the carousel is a region with keyboard support — arrow keys move between cards, Escape collapses an open panel — and every control carries an aria label. The open panel announces its content.

Motion: with reduced motion enabled, cards and illustrations render fully static with no idle animation. Idle animation also pauses while the carousel is off-screen. Opening and closing a card become instant.

Install note: the component is installed as several `vault-*.tsx` files into `components/ui` that must stay together — do not delete or rename individual files.
